## The short version

Amigology does not collect your personal data, does not store it on our servers, and does not use AI to analyze it. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account (on iPhone) or your phone's backup in your Google account (on Android), and, if you turn on Calendar sync, in your phone's Calendar.

## Information we collect

Amigology does not collect any personal information. When you first open the app, it asks for your first name so it can greet you. Your name, and the people, catch-ups, events, notes and reminders you add, are stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud account or, on Android, your phone's backup in your Google account (see below). We never receive any of it.

## Where your data is stored

### On iPhone {#on-iphone}

Your data is stored on your device and in an Amigology folder in your iCloud Drive, which is how it reaches your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. You can see this folder in the Files app. The files in it are encrypted so that only Amigology can open them.

Your iCloud data is protected by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you turn off iCloud Drive for Amigology, or aren't signed in to iCloud, your data stays only on your device.

### On Android {#on-android}

Your data is stored on your phone, in Amigology's private storage, which other apps can't read. Android's own backup keeps a copy of it, including the contact photos Amigology has copied (see below), in your Google account along with your other apps' data. Your phone backs up about once a day while it's charging and on Wi-Fi, and the backup is restored when you set up a new phone or reinstall Amigology from Google Play. A few settings, such as your reminder time and appearance, aren't included in the backup.

On Android, Amigology doesn't sync between devices: each phone keeps its own data. The backup is a Google service and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If you turn off backup in your phone's settings, your data stays only on your phone.

## No AI on your data

Amigology does not use artificial intelligence, machine learning or large language models on your data. Your data is never used to train models or to build a profile of you.

## Device permissions

Amigology asks for these permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working.

- **Notifications (optional).** Used for reminders, at a time you choose, when someone is due a check-in or has a birthday coming up. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Contacts (optional).** With your permission, Amigology reads your contacts so you can choose people to import, and so it can point out contacts you've added since you last looked. Only the people you choose are added to Amigology.
- **Calendar (optional).** Used only if you turn on Calendar sync (an Amigology Plus feature). See below.

For people linked to a contact, Amigology copies the contact's photo into the app and keeps it up to date, on every plan. Without contacts sync, the photo is the only thing it copies from your Contacts. Photos stay on the device they were copied to and are not stored in iCloud Drive. On Android, they're included in your phone's backup in your Google account.

If you turn on contacts sync for someone (an Amigology Plus feature), Amigology keeps their name, nickname, phone number, email, birthday and social profiles in step with their entry in your Contacts, in both directions. On Android, social profiles stay in Amigology, because Android's Contacts has no place for them. It may update that entry or create one if none exists. It never deletes anything from your Contacts.

Contact details you add to Amigology are stored with the rest of your data, on your device and in your iCloud Drive or, on Android, your phone's backup in your Google account. They are never sent to us.

If you turn on Calendar sync, Amigology creates an "Amigology" calendar in your phone's Calendar app and keeps your events in step with it, in both directions: each event's title, date and time, location and notes. Who an event is with stays in Amigology and is not added to the Calendar. Amigology does not read or change your other calendars or their events. The Amigology calendar is kept in the account your phone uses for new calendars, such as iCloud, and is governed by that service's privacy policy. On Android, the Amigology calendar is kept only on your phone, not in your Google account. Turning Calendar sync off leaves the entries already in the Calendar; you can delete the Amigology calendar in the Calendar app.

## Purchases, Apple and Google {#purchases-and-apple-services}

On iPhone, Amigology Plus is sold as a subscription through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

On Android, Amigology Plus is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we do not receive your payment details. Google Play gives us a record of each order, such as its order number, date and price and, for tax purposes, the country, state and postal code it was bought from. We use these records only for accounting, taxes and refunds. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google may send us crash reports. They describe your phone (such as its model and Android version) and the error, not the people or anything else you've added. Your use of Google Play is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

## Third parties, tracking and advertising

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Amigology has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

When you tap a place, Amigology opens it in your phone's maps app (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android), which then receives that place.

## Your control over your data

You can edit or delete anyone and anything in the app at any time.

To delete all of your Amigology data at once, open Amigology and go to Settings → Delete Amigology data. On iPhone, this deletes it from your device and from the Amigology folder in your iCloud Drive, and, if you use Calendar sync, deletes the Amigology calendar in your phone's Calendar. Your other devices delete their copy the next time they open Amigology. On Android, it deletes your data from your phone, along with the Amigology calendar, and your phone's next backup replaces the copy in your Google account. Either way, your phone's Contacts are not affected.

You can also delete the app, and then remove Amigology's data from iCloud: on your iPhone, go to Settings → \[your name\] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Amigology, or delete the Amigology folder in iCloud Drive in the Files app. If you used Calendar sync, delete the Amigology calendar in the Calendar app too.

If you uninstall Amigology on Android, its data is deleted from your phone, but your Google account may keep the last backup, which is restored if you reinstall Amigology. To remove that copy too, delete your data in Amigology and let your phone back up before you uninstall it, or delete your phone's backup in your Google account (which also deletes the backup of your other apps).

On iPhone, to stop syncing, turn off iCloud Drive for Amigology. On Android, to stop backing up, turn off backup in your phone's settings (this applies to all your apps). To stop Calendar sync, turn it off in Amigology's Settings.

Because we never have your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you.

## Children's privacy

Amigology is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

On iPhone, your data is protected by your device's passcode, your Apple Account's security and iCloud's encryption. Amigology also encrypts the files it keeps in your iCloud Drive, so they can't be read or changed outside the app.

On Android, your data is protected by your phone's screen lock and your Google account's security. On Android 9 and later, your phone's backup is encrypted using your screen lock PIN, pattern or password.
