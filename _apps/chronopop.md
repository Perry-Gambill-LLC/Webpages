## The short version

Chronopop does not collect your personal data, does not store it on our servers, and does not use AI to analyze it. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

## Information we collect

Chronopop does not collect any personal information. When you first open the app, it asks for your first name so it can greet you. Your name, and the people, catch-ups, notes and reminders you add, are stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud account (see below). We never receive any of it.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device and in a Chronopop folder in your iCloud Drive, which is how it reaches your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. You can see this folder in the Files app. The files in it are encrypted so that only Chronopop can open them.

Your iCloud data is protected by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you turn off iCloud Drive for Chronopop, or aren't signed in to iCloud, your data stays only on your device.

## No AI on your data

Chronopop does not use artificial intelligence, machine learning or large language models on your data. Your data is never used to train models or to build a profile of you.

## Device permissions

Chronopop asks for these permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working.

- **Notifications (optional).** Used for reminders when someone is due a check-in or has a birthday coming up. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Contacts (optional).** With your permission, Chronopop reads your contacts so you can choose people to import, and so it can point out contacts you've added since you last looked. Only the people you choose are added to Chronopop.

If you turn on contacts sync for someone (a Chronopop Plus feature), Chronopop keeps their name, nickname, phone number, email, birthday and social profiles in step with their entry in your Contacts, in both directions. It may update that entry or create one if none exists, and it copies the contact's photo into the app. It never deletes anything from your Contacts.

Contact details you add to Chronopop are stored with the rest of your data, on your device and in your iCloud Drive. They are never sent to us.

## Purchases and Apple services

Chronopop Plus is sold as a subscription through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

## Third parties, tracking and advertising

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Chronopop has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

## Your control over your data

You can edit or delete anyone and anything in the app at any time.

To delete all of your Chronopop data, delete the app, and then remove Chronopop's data from iCloud: on your iPhone, go to Settings → \[your name\] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Chronopop, or delete the Chronopop folder in iCloud Drive in the Files app.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud Drive for Chronopop.

Because we never have your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you.

## Children's privacy

Chronopop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Your data is protected by your device's passcode, your Apple Account's security and iCloud's encryption. Chronopop also encrypts the files it keeps in your iCloud Drive, so they can't be read or changed outside the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and mention significant changes in the app's release notes.
